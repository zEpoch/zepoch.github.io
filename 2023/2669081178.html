Calculate the complexity of cells in a region

Created2023-12-29|Updated2023-12-29|tech
|Word Count:800|Reading Time:5mins|Post Views:

In some spatial group atlas articles, we often see a picture, which is to calculate the complexity of cells in a region. We take an article published in Cell this year as an example to see how to calculate the complexity of cells in a region. The name of the article is Single-cell spatial transcriptome reveals cell-type organization in the macaque cortex, and the link to the article is: https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/S0092867423006797, there is such a picture in Figure II:

The meaning of I figure is the complexity distribution of each cell in different layers. The calculation of each cell's complexity is to take each cell as the center, 200 pixels as the radius, draw a circle, and calculate the number of cell types different from this cell in this circle.
